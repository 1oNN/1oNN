Hooked

A little crochet game. Follow the pattern, keep your tension, frog your mistakes.

How to play

  1. Make the stitch the pattern asks for. Press and hold its key: C chain, S single, H half double, D double, B bobble.
  2. Release in the green to pull the loop through at a neat tension. Too quick is loose, too long is tight. Loose stitches waste yarn.
  3. Turn at the end of each row with T, and change yarn with 1, 2 when the pattern says.
  4. Frog a wrong or wonky stitch with F to rip it back out and try again.
