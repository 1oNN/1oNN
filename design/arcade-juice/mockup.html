How to play

1 Make the stitch the pattern asks for

Press and hold its key or button: C chain, S single, H half double, D double, B bobble.

2 Release in the green

Too quick is loose, too long is tight. Loose stitches waste yarn. Neat in a row builds a combo.

3 Turn and change yarn

T turns the work at the end of a row; 1 and 2 swap yarn when the pattern says.

4 Frog your mistakes

A wrong stitch lands red with a ✕. Press F to rip it back out and carry on.
