How to play
  1. 01Make the stitch the pattern asks for.Press and hold its key: C chain, S single, H half double, D double, B bobble.
  2. 02Release in the green.Too quick is loose, too long is tight. Loose stitches waste yarn.
  3. 03Turn at the end of each row.T turns the work. Change yarn with 1 2 when the pattern says.
  4. 04Frog your mistakes.F rips the last stitch back out so you can try again.
